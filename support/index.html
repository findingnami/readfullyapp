Readfully Support

Help for the Readfully reading tracker on iPhone

Need a hand? Email hello@findingnami.com. I read every message and aim to reply within a few days.

It helps if you include your iPhone model, your iOS version, and the app version (Readfully › Settings › Version), and a screenshot if you can.

Common questions

Where are my books stored?

Only on your iPhone. Readfully has no accounts and no servers, so your library isn't uploaded anywhere. That also means it can't be recovered for you if the phone is lost, so back it up now and then.

How do I back up my books?

Open the My Books tab, tap the gear icon, then Settings › Back up your books. Save the file somewhere safe, like Files or iCloud Drive. To restore it later, go to Settings › Restore from a backup and pick the file. Restoring adds books you don't have and never changes or deletes the ones you do.

I got a new phone. How do I move my books?

On the old phone, make a backup (see above) and save it to iCloud Drive or send it to yourself. On the new phone, install Readfully and restore it from Settings › Restore from a backup.

Can I open my books in a spreadsheet?

Yes. Settings › Export as spreadsheet (CSV) creates a file that opens in Numbers, Excel, or Google Sheets. It is for reading and analysis. Use the backup file to restore.

The barcode scanner doesn't work.

Check that camera access is on: iPhone Settings › Readfully › Camera. Hold the phone steady with the whole barcode in view, using the barcode on the back cover. If it still won't scan, tap Type ISBN instead, or search by title. Scanning needs an iPhone with a camera, and it doesn't work in the iOS Simulator.

A book isn't found, or the details are wrong.

Readfully looks books up in Open Library, a free community library catalog, so some books or editions are missing or have different page counts. You can always add a book yourself with Enter it manually, and every detail, including the cover, can be edited later.

Does Readfully need an internet connection?

Only to look books up by search or scan. Everything else works offline.

How do I delete my data?

Settings › Delete all my data erases everything from the app. Deleting the app also removes its data from your phone. I don't hold a copy, so make a backup first if you might want it back.

Does Readfully have ads or collect my information?

No. There are no ads, accounts, or analytics. See the Privacy Policy for exactly what is and isn't sent.
