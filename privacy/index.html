Privacy Policy

Readfully for iPhone · Effective October 18, 2026

The short version: your books stay on your phone. Readfully has no accounts, no ads, no analytics, and no tracking. The only time anything leaves your phone is when you search for a book or scan its barcode, and then only the search text or ISBN goes to Open Library.

Who we are

Readfully is made by Maria Rachel, under the name Finding Nami ("I", "me"). You can reach me at hello@findingnami.com.

What stays on your phone

Everything you enter or create in Readfully is stored only on your device: the books on your shelves, titles, authors, series, ISBNs, page and chapter counts, your progress and reading activity, ratings, notes, dates, covers, reading goals, and settings. I do not have access to any of it. Readfully has no accounts and no servers of its own, so there is nothing for me to collect, store, or sell.

What is sent when you look up a book

When you search by title, author, or ISBN, or scan a barcode, Readfully sends a request to Open Library (openlibrary.org and covers.openlibrary.org), a free service run by the Internet Archive. This only happens when you type a search or scan a barcode. Nothing is sent at launch or in the background.

A request includes:

  • the title, author, or ISBN you searched for or scanned;
  • the identifier of the cover image, when a cover is downloaded;
  • technical information every internet request carries, such as your IP address, which Open Library receives like any website would; and
  • a short app identifier (the app name, version, and a contact email, hello@findingnami.com) that Open Library asks apps to send so they can reach the developer if something goes wrong. It does not identify you.

Open Library handles that information under its own policies, which are published by the Internet Archive at archive.org/about/terms.php. Nothing else from your library is sent, and I do not receive any of these requests.

The camera

If you tap the scan button and allow camera access, the camera is used only to read a barcode while the scanner is open. The image is processed on your device. Nothing is recorded, saved, or sent anywhere. You can turn camera access off at any time in Settings › Readfully on your iPhone, and you can still add books by typing.

Photos

If you choose a cover from your photo library, Readfully receives only the single photo you pick, using Apple's photo picker. It is resized and saved on your device with that book. Readfully cannot see the rest of your photo library.

Backups and exports

Readfully can create a backup file or a spreadsheet (CSV) of your books. These files are created on your device and shared only where you choose, such as Files or iCloud Drive. Readfully does not upload them. If you use iCloud Backup or an encrypted computer backup for your iPhone, Apple may include Readfully's data in that device backup, under Apple's terms.

No ads, no tracking, no analytics

Readfully contains no advertising, no analytics or tracking tools, and no third-party code that collects data. I do not build a profile of you and I do not share or sell any information, because I do not have any.

Children

Readfully is not directed to children and collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Deleting your data

You are in control of your data. In Readfully, Settings › Delete all my data erases every book, note, rating, activity entry, and goal from the app. Deleting the app from your iPhone also removes its data from the device. Because I hold no copy, I cannot recover data you delete, so make a backup first if you might want it back.

Changes to this policy

If Readfully's behavior changes in a way that affects your privacy, for example if a feature is added that sends different information, I will update this page and the date above, and describe the change in the app's release notes.

Contact

Questions about privacy? Email hello@findingnami.com.
